PocketPantry FAQ Reference

Use this information to answer common pre-visit questions briefly and confidently.
If a caller asks something outside this document, do not guess. Say Arthur can confirm it in person.

Overview
- PocketPantry provides smart vending and hybrid vending solutions for businesses and facilities.
- Arthur is the local owner-operator and handles in-person visits.

Common Answers

1. Cost to install
- There is no cost to the location for the machine, installation, stocking, or ongoing maintenance.
- PocketPantry earns revenue from product sales.

2. Installation timeline
- After a location is qualified and the menu is finalized, delivery and full stocking typically happen within 7 to 10 business days.

3. Product gets stuck
- Smart machines use vision sensors and weight sensing so the transaction only completes when the item is successfully delivered.
- Hybrid machines use drop sensors to guarantee delivery or trigger an immediate refund.

4. Payment methods
- Machines accept Apple Pay, Google Pay, and secure chip-enabled credit and debit cards.
- Some machines can also accept cash.

5. Loyalty program
- Users can earn rewards and discounts through the Monyx Mobile App.

6. Restocking frequency
- Inventory is monitored remotely with real-time alerts.
- Machines are typically serviced 1 to 2 times per week, or more often for higher-volume locations.

7. Healthy or specific product requests
- PocketPantry can curate the menu around location preferences.
- This can include high-protein snacks, gluten-free options, healthier items, organic items, and classic favorites.

8. Maintenance response
- As a local operator, PocketPantry aims to dispatch service within 24 hours when a machine needs maintenance.

9. Internal announcements on screens
- Qualified machines can display internal announcements or safety messages on the digital screen at no extra charge.

10. Advertising on machines
- PocketPantry offers advertising options for local and regional brands.
- Approved creative can be launched on the network after onboarding.

11. Digital vs static advertising
- Digital media uses the machine’s screen for video or static image ads.
- Static media uses physical branding like decals or full wraps on the machine.

12. Digital display pricing
- Digital display advertising can be priced as low as 3 dollars per day per machine.

13. Decal and wrap pricing
- Static media pricing depends on the location and traffic profile.
- Typical pricing ranges from 50 to 100 dollars per month per machine.

14. Advertising contract term
- Advertising placements generally require a one-year commitment.

Answer Style
- Keep answers short and natural.
- Do not over-explain.
- After answering, return to scheduling or qualifying the visit.
