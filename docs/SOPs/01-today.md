# 01 — Today

**What it's for:** Your morning starting point. Shows everything you need to know about *right now* in one screen.

## How to get there

Sidebar → **Today** (top of the list). Also opens by default when you log in.

## What you see

### Top tiles (left to right)

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Today's Revenue · LIVE** | Total revenue across all machines so far today. Updates as new sales come in. "vs yesterday" shows the day-over-day change. |
| **Machines Active** | How many machines are healthy vs offline. Example: `7 / 8` means 7 healthy, 1 offline. |
| **Open Alerts** | Active alerts (low stock, machine offline, price changes needed). `14 high severity` = 14 critical ones. |
| **Warehouse Value** | Total dollar value of stock sitting in your warehouse + count of items low. |

### Today's Refill Stops

Lists machines that need refilling first (sorted by urgency). Each row shows the machine name and how many low items it has. Red dot = needs attention soon.

**What to do with it:** Plan your refill route — start with the machine that has the most low items.

### Warehouse Restock

Summary of what you need to buy to keep the warehouse stocked. Shows total restock cost + how many products are below threshold.

**What to do with it:** Click through to **Inventory → Buy List** to generate a purchase order.

### Price Change Review

Top 3 products with pending price changes. Shows current cost vs suggested vending price.

**What to do with it:** Click through to **Pricing** to approve or reject each suggestion.

### New Location Reply (if shown)

Latest reply from a lead (email). Includes their intent (interested, not interested, etc).

**What to do with it:** Open **Email Pipeline** or **Lead Dashboard** to act on it.

## Common workflows

### Morning routine (2 minutes)

1. Open Today
2. Glance at **Today's Revenue** — is it tracking with yesterday?
3. Check **Open Alerts** — anything urgent?
4. Look at **Today's Refill Stops** — is the route plan still good?

### "Numbers look off" troubleshooting

If Today's Revenue seems low or hasn't moved:

1. **Wait 30-60 seconds** — the page auto-syncs in the background after every load
2. Click **Refresh** on the Machines page to force a sync
3. Compare to the **Machines page** Total Revenue tile — they should agree

## Common questions

**Q: Today's Revenue and the Machines page Total Revenue are different. Why?**
A: Today's Revenue shows just today. Machines Total Revenue is all-time cumulative. They measure different things on purpose.

**Q: What if a machine is offline?**
A: Offline = no data from Nayax in the last 3 days. Check the machine in person — it may need to be plugged back in or rebooted.

**Q: Can I customize this page?**
A: Not currently. The layout is fixed but the data updates live.
